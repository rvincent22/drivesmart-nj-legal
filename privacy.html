Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the DriveSmart NJ app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Xavier Vincent (collectively, the "Application"). Xavier Vincent is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects the following information when you create an account or use the Application:

  • Email address
  • Name
  • Profile photo

These are collected when you sign in using Google. The Application also collects the content of messages you send to the AI Tutor feature, which are processed to generate responses. This data is retained and used as described in this privacy policy.

Artificial Intelligence

The Application uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to power the AI Tutor feature. Messages you send to the AI Tutor are transmitted to OpenAI's API for processing and response generation. This processing is performed in accordance with this privacy policy and applicable laws. OpenAI's privacy policy is available at https://openai.com/privacy. If you have questions about the AI features or data processing, please contact the Service Provider.
